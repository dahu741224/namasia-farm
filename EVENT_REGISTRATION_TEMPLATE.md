# EVENT_REGISTRATION_TEMPLATE.md

## Purpose
Reusable workflow for Namasiya Small Farmer event applications.

## Before filling any form
1. Read `AGENTS.md`.
2. Confirm the official form is still accepting responses.
3. Confirm deadline, dates, location, operating hours, fees, deposit, revenue share, power/fire rules, water/waste rules, tableware rules, vehicle rules, and qualification requirements.
4. Check Google Calendar conflicts when available.

## Fixed data source
Use the Google Drive document `那瑪夏小農夫｜市集報名共用資料庫` for confirmed brand data, contact data, social links, menu, vehicle, equipment, and photo references.
Do not guess missing official data.

## Fill directly when confirmed
- Brand name and introduction
- Confirmed contact details
- Confirmed social links
- Confirmed menu and price range
- Confirmed vehicle data
- Confirmed equipment data
- Confirmed photo categories

## Stop and ask the user only for
- New legal or major agreement terms
- Payment, transfer, deposit, or added fees
- Uncertain power/fire/equipment data
- Missing official information
- Final submission

Do not stop for every field. Group all questions from the same page into one message.

## Brand positioning
Use: `原鄉小農蔬菜 × 原民山味 × 現場燒烤料理`

## Photos
Prioritize:
- Brand image: logo, full brand visual, people with branded stall
- Main products: grilled meats, sausage, vegetables, meal sets
- Stall appearance: full frontage, menu, service counter, lighting, real operating scenes

## Final review before submit
Summarize once:
- Event name/date/location
- Fees/deposit/share
- Payment deadline
- Products/prices
- Power/fire setup
- Rentals
- Tableware needs
- Vehicle data
- Uploaded photos
- Key rules

Then ask: `現在只差最後送出，是否確認提交？`
Do not submit before confirmation.

## Success status
Only mark success after the form shows a recorded-response success message.
- 🟡 已送件｜等待錄取
- 🟢 確定出攤
- 🔴 未錄取／取消

## After submission
Save event name, date, place, application date, official form URL, fees, restrictions, notification method, payment deadline, organizer contact, and proof of successful submission.
Update Google Calendar status when possible.

## Usage-saving rules
- Reuse this file and `AGENTS.md` instead of re-explaining the workflow.
- Read only the designated Drive reference first; do not search the whole Drive unless needed.
- Reuse confirmed answers.
- Ask grouped questions instead of one-by-one questions.
- Avoid restarting long sessions unless necessary.

## Standard Codex command
`依照 AGENTS.md 與 EVENT_REGISTRATION_TEMPLATE.md，幫我報名這個活動：<官方報名網址>。固定資料讀 Google Drive 的「那瑪夏小農夫｜市集報名共用資料庫」。已確認資料直接填，只有重大條款、付款、重要缺件與最後送出前再問我。`
